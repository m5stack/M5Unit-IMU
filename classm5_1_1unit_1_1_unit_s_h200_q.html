Detailed Description

Senodia SH200Q 6-axis IMU (accel + gyro + temp). No standalone GROVE unit exists; it is the built-in IMU of the earliest (2019.3) M5StickC and of the 2019.7 M5Stack Fire (there paired with a discrete BMM150 magnetometer), on the internal I2C bus at 0x6C.

Warning
Field reports (and the chip's EOL status) suggest the gyroscope output is of poor quality (large per-axis zero offsets) and that the accel and gyro axis systems may not even agree with each other or with the datasheet – verify the orientation on hardware and expect to need gyro zero-offset calibration. The accelerometer is the dependable half of this chip.
