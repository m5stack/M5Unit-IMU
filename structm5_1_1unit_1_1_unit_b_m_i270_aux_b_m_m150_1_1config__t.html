bmm150::Preset m5::unit::UnitBMI270AuxBMM150::config_t::aux_mag_preset {bmm150::Preset::Regular}

Accuracy/power preset begin() seeds the magnetometer's repetition from; change it afterwards with writeAuxMagPreset()/writeAuxMagRepetition(). HighAccuracy cannot be used here: its measurement takes 49.3ms, longer than the 40ms period of the bridge's fixed 25Hz poll, so startPeriodicMeasurement() rejects it instead of letting the data registers go stale
