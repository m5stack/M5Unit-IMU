Detailed Description

One MAG3110 sample: a 7-byte burst from DR_STATUS (0x00)

raw = {DR_STATUS, XMSB, XLSB, YMSB, YLSB, ZMSB, ZLSB}. Axes are big-endian. adj_*() rescale the native 0.10 µT/LSB output to the library-wide 16 LSB/µT convention (so imu_convert_param_t::mag_res stays a uniform 1/16, matching the other magnetometers).
