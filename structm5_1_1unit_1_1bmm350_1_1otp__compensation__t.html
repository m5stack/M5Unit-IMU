Detailed Description

Per-chip OTP compensation coefficients (read out at boot from the sensor's one-time-programmable memory), consumed by compensate_temp() and compensate_mag(). All-zero (default-constructed, aside from t0) is a neutral / pass-through configuration.

Formulas re-implemented (not copied) from the Bosch BMM350_SensorAPI (bmm350_get_compensated_mag_xyz_temp_data(), BSD-3-Clause), verified against https://github.com/boschsensortec/BMM350_SensorAPI/blob/main/bmm350.c
