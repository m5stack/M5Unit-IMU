Detailed Description

Explicit complementary filter with proportional-integral feedback (Mahony et al. 2008)

Implements the explicit complementary filter on SO(3) from Mahony, Hamel and Pflimlin's "Nonlinear Complementary Filters on the Special Orthogonal Group" (2008), re-derived for this repository's coordinate/quaternion convention (see filter_base.hpp): body Z-up, v_world = q.rotate(v_body). Each update() integrates the gyro rate after correcting it with a proportional-integral term driven by the body-frame cross-product error between the measured accel/mag directions and the predicted reference direction(s) (gravity, and – when a magnetometer sample is supplied – the horizontal magnetic field recomputed every step from the current attitude estimate).
