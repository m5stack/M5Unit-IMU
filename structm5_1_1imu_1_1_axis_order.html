Detailed Description

Chip-frame-to-body-frame axis remap: a permutation plus a per-output-axis sign flip.

Applied by AhrsController::update() to every accel/gyro/mag sample from a given IMUDataSource, right after raw-to-physical conversion and before auto-calibration offsets are subtracted (see AhrsController::writeAxisOrder()/readAxisOrder()). Mirrors M5Unified's internal_axisorder_t, operating directly on physical-unit float triplets.

The default (AxisPermutation::XYZ, no inversion) is the identity transform, i.e. exactly what a source already reports in its own chip frame.
