Detailed Description

Unit Mini IMU-Pro (U171): BMI270 (0x68, fixed – no address switch) + BMP280 (0x76) on the GROVE main bus, with a BMM150 magnetometer wired to the BMI270's AUX interface (ASDX/ASCX), not to the main bus. The predecessor of Unit DoF9/DoF10, with a BMM150/BMP280 instead of the BMM350/SPA06.

Because the BMM150 hangs off the BMI270's AUX bridge – the same topology as CoreS3 and AtomS3R – imu() alone provides all nine axes (UnitBMI270AuxBMM150 probes and drives the AUX BMM150, and reports Capability::Mag once it answers). Add imu() to an AhrsController; read baro() directly (it is not AHRS-fused). The barometer driver (UnitBMP280) comes from M5Unit-ENV.
