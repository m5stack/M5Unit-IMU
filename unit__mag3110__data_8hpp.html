enum class m5::unit::mag3110::DataRate : uint8_t
strong

Output data rate select (REG_CTRL_REG1 DR[7:5])

Each value is named after the output rate it gives at OverSampling::X16, which is how the datasheet labels them (Table 32); a larger oversampling ratio divides that down. DR on its own fixes the ADC rate and with it the supply current, from 900µA at Rate80Hz to 8.6µA at Rate0_63Hz

◆ OverSampling

enum class m5::unit::mag3110::OverSampling : uint8_t
strong

Over sampling ratio (REG_CTRL_REG1 OS[4:3])

OS on its own fixes the noise floor: 0.40µT rms at X16 down to 0.25µT rms at X128. It also divides the output rate, so most output rates are reachable from several DataRate/OverSampling pairs whose current and noise differ widely – 10Hz for instance is available at 900/550/275/137.5µA with 0.25/0.30/0.35/0.40µT rms. That trade-off is why the two fields are exposed separately
