Detailed Description

One AK8963 sample: an 8-byte burst from ST1 (0x02) plus the device ASA trim.

raw = {ST1, HXL, HXH, HYL, HYH, HZL, HZH, ST2}. Axes are little-endian. adj_*() apply the per-axis sensitivity adjustment and rescale to the library-wide 16 LSB/µT convention (so imu_convert_param_t::mag_res stays a uniform 1/16).
